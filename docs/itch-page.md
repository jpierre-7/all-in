Twenty-five years ago Lucky Jack sat down at the Big Shots Table with everything he owned, lost it in an hour, and then bet his son. Tonight he walks back in to collect.

**All In** is a card-combat roguelike. You walk up through a casino, floor by floor, and every encounter is a hand of cards against the House. Draw seven, play up to five; the cards you play stack into your Hand. Clear the House Edge and the extra comes off the enemy's chips. Fall short and the difference comes off yours.

Two card types bend that. A **Streak** doubles if the card before it had a Tell. An **All In** burns another card from your hand and takes its chips. Same five cards, different order, half the value: the order you play in is the skill.

Once your Hand is final you can **Push Your Luck** on a coin that's 45/55 the House's way. Bosses pay in a perk you pick one of two, with no going back. Every few turns the Blinds rise.

Then the House. It reads your Hand after your fourth card and sets its Edge one chip above it. Your fifth card is the only one it can't see.

There's a tutorial in the lobby that deals a fixed hand and walks you through everything in eight keypresses. Start there.

### Controls

- **Space** starts. **Any key** turns a page.
- **1–7** or **click** plays a card. An All In waits for a second card to burn.
- **Enter** shows your Hand. **P** pushes your luck, **H** holds.
- **I** opens the glossary at the table. **M** mutes the music. **Esc** leaves the Arcade.

### Running it

Linux: unzip, then run `all-in.sh` (it launches from its own folder so the game can find `assets/`). Built with Rust and Bevy 0.19.
