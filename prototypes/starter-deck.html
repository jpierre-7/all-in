All In: starter deck prototype

Question: does this fixed starter deck, played as Draw 7 / Play 5, produce both Payouts and Whiffs against a plausible House Edge, and do Streak chains and All In sacrifices fire often enough to matter? Edit the deck at the bottom; everything above re-reads it.

Rules, as in src/combat/duel.rs: cards resolve left to right. Streak doubles its own Stack if the card played immediately before it this turn had any Tell. All In burns a card you name from the rest of your Draw and adds its Stack; the burned card is discarded, not played, so it doesn't count for Streak. Leftover Draw carries over and refills to 7; the discard reshuffles when the deck runs dry. Whiff damage = House Edge − Hand. Rising Blinds: +2 to House Edge every 2 turns.
